Experimental equation for the speed of propagation:
$$
v = C \sqrt{ \frac{tdg}{h} }
$$
Where $t$ is the thickness of the dominoes, $d$ is the spacing, $h$ is the height of the dominoes, and $g$ is the acceleration from gravity. $C$ is a proportionality constant to be determined from curve fitting.

After each sample, the propagation time is converted to the propagation speed according to the relation:
$$
v= \frac{\Delta d}{\Delta t} = \frac{n(t+d)}{\Delta t} = \frac{n(t+Nt)}{\Delta t} = \frac{(N+1)nt}{\Delta t}
$$
Where $n$ is the number of dominoes being measured, $N$ is the spacing (as a multiple of the domino thickness) and $\Delta t$ is the propagation time.

$$
v = \frac{(N+1)nt}{\Delta t} = \frac{(2+1)(5)(7.5)}{0.1487} \approx 756.56
$$
